Theory

Xerophytes show sunken stomata, thick cuticle and fleshy stems; hydrophytes show broad thin leaves, aerenchyma and reduced roots. The FSM scores observed traits against the expected set for the chosen habitat.

Adaptation score = 100 × matched traits / expected traits (deterministic FSM)

Precautions & Standard Errors

  • Observe both morphological and anatomical traits before concluding.
  • Compare with a known reference specimen of each habitat.
  • Do not judge habitat from a single trait; use the full trait set.
