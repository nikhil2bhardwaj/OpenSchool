Theory

Bohr postulates: electrons revolve in stable orbits with angular momentum mvr = n*hbar, and radiate only during transitions. Energy of level n in hydrogen:

En = -13.6 / n^2 eV

Photon energy for transition ni -> nf: dE = |Eni - Enf| in eV. Wavelength:

lambda = h*c / dE, with h = 6.626e-34 J-s, c = 3e8 m/s, e = 1.602e-19 J/eV

Lyman series: nf = 1 (UV). Balmer series: nf = 2 (visible). Paschen: nf = 3 (IR). If ni = nf there is no transition and no photon.

Precautions and Standard Errors

  • Use the correct sign: bound levels are negative, En = -13.6/n^2 eV.
  • Convert eV to joule with e = 1.602e-19 before using lambda = hc/dE.
  • Report wavelength in nm (1 m = 1e9 nm) and match the series to the spectral region.
